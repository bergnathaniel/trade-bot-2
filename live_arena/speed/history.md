# Speed test history: 8 weeks, 2026-07-20 to 2026-09-07

Paper money only. The weekly report's rules (SPEED_TEST.md), applied to every bot in every week. All of these weeks are practice, so none count toward a streak.

## Could this be luck?

- 888 contestants (a bot in one market at one candle size) had 7,104 weekly chances and passed 51 times (0.72%).
- If every pass were luck at that rate, about 1.2 contestants would pass 2 or more of the 8 weeks by chance. 11 did.
- The controls built to have no edge (Coin Flip, Independent Coin Flip, Opposite Day, Random Entry, 1-Hour Hold, Random Entry, 24-Hour Hold, Random Entry, 2:1 Exits, Random Entry, 8-Hour Hold) passed 2 of 224 weekly chances.
